CASE 07 · MARKET ENTRY · KELLOGG CONSULTING CLUB 2019 D4 · ~40 MIN
Low-cost carrier enters the Nordic market
BACKGROUND

Skanwing is a Copenhagen-based low-cost carrier operating 42 narrow-body aircraft on intra-European routes, with €1.1B in annual revenue and unit costs among the lowest in the region. Management is weighing entry into the Nordic domestic market — Norway, Sweden and Finland — where incumbent full-service carriers hold roughly 80% of seats.

THE ASK

The CEO has asked: should Skanwing enter the Nordic domestic market, and if so, how? The board's bar is €120M of incremental revenue within three years, profitable by year two.

CASE 07 · ANSWER KEY INTERVIEWER ONLY
Quant chain & expected so-what
—Volume: 14M pax × 8% share = 1.12M pax by year three.
—Fare: €95 × (1 − 15%) = €80.75 average one-way.
—Revenue: 1.12M × €80.75 ≈ €90M — €30M short of the €120M bar.
—So-what: ticket revenue alone fails the goal → brainstorm must close the gap (ancillaries ~€12–15 per pax, cargo, trunk frequency, partnerships).
